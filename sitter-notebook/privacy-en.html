Sitter Notebook — Privacy policy and subscription terms
Effective: 28 October 2026
Contact: tripexsupport@gmail.com

Sitter Notebook is a business notebook for independent adult pet sitters. It is not a pet-sitting marketplace or a veterinary service. The developer can be contacted at the email above.

YOUR NOTEBOOK
Household details, contact numbers, addresses, pet routines, bookings, visit notes, photos, reviewed reports and manual balance/session records are stored on your device. The notebook database and processed photos are encrypted using keys held with Android secure storage. We do not operate a cloud notebook server or receive those records through the advertising or subscription integration. We do not request contact-list access, background location or access codes. Do not enter key/alarm codes or medical dosing instructions.

You can choose photos or use the camera for a visit. Processed copies are kept in private app storage with metadata removed. Originals in your gallery remain outside the app. Encryption reduces risk but cannot protect a device that is already compromised or copies you export.

SHARING AND BACKUPS
Reports are shared only when you review them and select a receiving app in Android's share chooser. That app and the recipient then handle the shared content under their own policies. CSV exports are readable and may contain household contacts, addresses and financial records. Portable backups are encrypted with your chosen passphrase. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Keep backups somewhere you control; uninstalling, clearing app storage or losing the device can lose the notebook. Pro is not cloud backup or synchronization.

ADS AND CONSENT
The Free plan can display Google AdMob banner ads. Google and its advertising service providers may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve their services. The app does not attach household names, addresses, pet information, visit notes, photos or notebook ledgers to ad requests. We do not enable precise-location collection or personalized customer audience lists.

Google's consent platform determines whether ads can be requested and presents choices where required. If available, reopen these choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Android also provides advertising privacy controls in device settings. Pro removes banner ads. We do not request new ads while verified Pro access is active. Data already received by Google remains subject to Google's retention policy.

Google privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Google Play handles payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and provides Pro access using an anonymous app-user identifier, purchase tokens/history, subscription status, product identifiers and relevant store/locale information. No name, email, phone number, advertising identifier or notebook content is supplied to RevenueCat as a customer attribute. We do not receive payment-card details. Network processing by these providers may occur outside your country and uses encrypted transport.

RevenueCat privacy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
Google Play payments: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

Free allows 30 non-cancelled visit instances per calendar month according to each visit's business-local date. Recurring bookings count once per visit. Pro allows unlimited new bookings and removes ads. Existing visits, reports, records, backup, restoration and CSV export remain accessible after expiry. New bookings and moves into a full month are limited on Free; already booked visits are retained.

Monthly or annual subscriptions renew automatically at the Google Play price displayed for that period unless cancelled in Google Play before renewal. No free trial is offered at launch. Payment is charged when you confirm through Google Play. Cancellation stops future renewal, and access continues for the paid period while verified by the store. Google Play's cancellation/refund rules apply. Restore purchases with the Google Play account that bought Pro; an internet connection is needed for purchases, restoration, ads and subscription checks. Access is granted only for verified active subscriptions, including a valid cached entitlement until its recorded expiry. Pending payments do not unlock Pro.

Manage subscriptions: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

RETENTION AND DELETION
Notebook records remain locally until you remove them. Settings → Delete notebook removes local notebook records, private photos and retained recovery copies. Copies previously exported, backed up, shared, or stored by another app are outside this deletion; remove them separately. Deleting the notebook or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Use Manage or cancel in Google Play.

Google and RevenueCat retain billing, fraud-prevention and service records according to their policies and applicable obligations. Email tripexsupport@gmail.com to request help with subscription-data access/deletion. We may need information to locate and verify the request; do not send notebook backups, passwords, home addresses or payment-card details. We cannot delete another service's records directly, and some transaction records may need to be retained. The app has no developer account registration.

SUPPORT, CHILDREN AND UPDATES
If you email support, your email address and message are processed by the email provider to answer your request. This app is intended for adult business use and does not target children. We do not add analytics, crash-reporting or third-party audience SDKs beyond the disclosed ad, consent and purchase services. This policy may change as the app changes; the effective date above identifies this version. Contact tripexsupport@gmail.com about privacy or subscriptions.
