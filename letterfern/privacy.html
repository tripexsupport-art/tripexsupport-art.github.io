Letterfern Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. Developer: Raven Ho. Contact: tripexsupport@gmail.com.

Word Search: Letterfern is an Android word-search game currently being prepared for release. This policy describes its implemented local gameplay and planned advertising and optional analytics. The release configuration and native integrations are still being verified; this page does not announce public availability.

Gameplay and local progress

No game account is required. Puzzles, selected words, hint credits, completion history and settings are saved on your device. Core puzzles can be played offline. Local saves remain until you reset them in Settings, remove app data or uninstall the app. There is no cloud progress sync. Resetting gameplay does not reset advertising privacy choices.

Advertising

The release is designed to use Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads at completed-puzzle breaks. No ad is required to continue solving. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google SDK connections use encryption in transit.

Required consent choices are presented through Google's User Messaging Platform before affected ad requests. Advertising privacy options are available in Settings when required. Ad availability depends on your region, consent and network conditions. You can also use Android's advertising-ID controls. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics are off by default and can be enabled or disabled in Settings. When enabled and configured, Google Analytics for Firebase receives puzzle-start/resume/completion, word-found, hint and ad-diagnostic events. Event fields include puzzle mode, theme, difficulty, content/generator version, attempt identifier and UTC event time. Google's SDK also processes app-instance, device and app information. This helps the developer understand gameplay and return visits. The app does not collect your contacts, photos or precise GPS location.

Disabling the switch stops future optional gameplay reporting; it does not automatically erase previously received reports. Advertising consent is managed separately. The app's analytics adapter denies advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization signals. See Firebase privacy information. Analytics setup is pending for this prelaunch version.

Support, retention and requests

If you contact support, the developer receives your email and the information you choose to include, and uses it to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details. Local gameplay is not uploaded to a developer game server. Google handles SDK data under its service policies and configured retention settings. Email support to request access or deletion where applicable; include only enough information to identify the request. Deleting local progress does not itself delete data already held by Google.

Audience and policy changes

Letterfern is intended for adult players and is not marketed to children. This policy will be updated if features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Native SDK inventory, consent behavior and reporting configuration must be reconciled with this notice before release.
